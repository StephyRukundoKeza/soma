# SOMA: Scoring Rubric

Each answer is scored from 0 to 2 on four criteria (maximum 8).

| Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Correctness** | Matches the book's answer | Partly correct or incomplete | Wrong or invented |
| **Grounding** | Cites the right book and page | Cites the right book, wrong page | No citation, or the wrong book |
| **Language** | Right language, natural, no errors | Right language, small errors | Wrong language or hard to understand |
| **Clarity for a 15-17-year-old** | Clear, short, suitable | Understandable but long or complex | Confusing or unsuitable |

For questions that are not in the books, a correct refusal ("This is not in your book", in the learner's language) scores 8; any invented answer scores 0.

Two educators score every answer independently; I report how often they agree.
